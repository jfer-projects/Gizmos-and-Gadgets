# Accessibility and child safety

## Accessibility rules
- Text pairs meet 4.5:1 in Day and Night Shift, and 7:1 in High Contrast. Meaningful borders and focus rings meet 3:1.
- Nothing relies on color alone. Win and loss, families, torque and speed each also carry a word, icon or pattern.
- Read-aloud is available for every instruction, hint and explanation. Icons have text labels.
- Body text is 18px on phones and scales with the OS text size up to 200 percent without clipping.
- Every puzzle has a non-drag alternative: tap a piece, then tap its target.
- One-handed play: primary controls sit in the lower corners. No two-finger gestures are required.
- Reduced motion turns off ambient gear rotation, confetti and screen shake; nothing shakes by default.
- No timers on puzzles. Races are deterministic and can be replayed without penalty.
- Focus order and a visible `focus-ring` support switch control and controllers.

## Child-safety design rules
- No ads, no third-party analytics, no accounts, no chat, no push notifications.
- Any link out, store review prompt or purchase sits behind the Parent Gate.
- Parent Gate: an adult-level task, such as typing numbers shown as words in a random order. It never defaults an age and never asks a child's age. Review the exact mechanism against current Apple and Google guidance before release.
- Purchases use the platform's Ask to Buy or parental controls; nothing is consumable.
- Store data only on the device. Do not collect names, photos, voice or location.
