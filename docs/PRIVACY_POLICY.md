Tinker Track is a game for children about nine to eleven. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about your child.

## What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our servers, or send anywhere any information about you or your child. It has no accounts, no chat, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no links to other sites.

## What stays on the device

The game saves your child's progress on the device: which crates are solved, stars earned, the last gears chosen, settings such as sound and text size, and the total time played. This file never leaves the device. You can erase it at any time from Settings, then "Erase my progress". Removing the game also removes it.

## Permissions

The game asks for one permission on Android: to vibrate the device for feedback, which can be switched off in Settings. It does not use the internet, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

## Reading aloud

If "Read aloud" is on, the game asks the device's built-in voice to speak the text on screen. That is handled by your device's own speech service under your device's settings.

## Parent zone

The Parent zone shows progress and is protected by a question meant for adults. It does not ask for your child's age and stores nothing extra.

## Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side. If this ever changes (for example an optional purchase), we will update this policy first, ask a parent before anything is collected, and follow the children's privacy laws that apply, including COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in Europe.
