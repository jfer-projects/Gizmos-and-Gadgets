# Roadmap

Ordered by what most reduces risk to a real release.

## Next (before any child plays it)

1. **Playtest with 9 to 11 year olds.** Watch, do not help. Questions: do they find the ratio idea themselves, is the first stall frustrating or interesting, do they understand the pest, can they read the text.
2. **Put it on a phone.** Set up the Android and iOS export (see `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`), check the stick, tap targets, frame rate and the device voice.
3. **Final art and fonts.** Use `docs/design-system/art-direction.md`. Bundle Bricolage Grotesque, Atkinson Hyperlegible and JetBrains Mono (all open licences) and record them in `ASSETS.md`.
4. **Legal review.** Privacy policy, kids-app compliance, trademark search for the name.

## Then

5. **Drag-and-snap builder** with a frame, wheels and power source, so the gear choice sits inside a visible build.
6. **More puzzle depth.** Pulleys and forces. Two-stage gear trains. Three difficulty tiers generated from templates.
7. **Screen-reader support** when Godot supports it. Until then, keep read-aloud and non-drag puzzles.
8. **Portrait menus and tablet layouts.**
9. **Localisation.** Move every string out of the code.
10. **Free Build sandbox, vehicle naming and colours, local profiles.**
11. **Teacher one-pagers** for each concept, with standards codes.

## Ideas, not committed

- A weekly challenge course with the same physics for everyone (no accounts needed if the seed is the date).
- A replay of your best run as a ghost.
- Optional paid full version behind the parent gate, with the free demo covering the first family.
