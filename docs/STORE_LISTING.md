## Long description

Tinker Track is a puzzle-racing game where your gears decide the race.

Open crates in the workshop and solve small science puzzles to earn new gears and parts. Then build your drivetrain: choose a motor gear and a wheel gear, watch the gear ratio change, and race a rival over hills, mud, wind and clouds.

Lose a race and the game shows you exactly why. Maybe your car stalled on the hill, or your wheels topped out on the straight. You get a clear reason, a hint, and a one-tap retry. Getting it wrong is how you learn how gears work.

- 15 courses across cars, green-energy vehicles and planes
- 12 crate puzzles: gears, circuits, magnets, energy sources and levers
- A codex that collects every idea you learn
- A Parent zone that shows what your child has learned and which school science standards it matches
- Made for ages 9 to 11

For families: no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no chat and no links to other sites. It works offline and collects no personal information. Includes Night Shift and High Contrast looks, large text, reduced motion, and read-aloud.
